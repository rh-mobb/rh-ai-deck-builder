# Deck Design Doc: Why Hire Paul Czarkowski

## Core Thesis
Paul brings deep cloud architecture expertise and a track record of scaling complex infrastructure—hiring him means access to someone who can unblock your biggest platform challenges.

## Know — Feel — Do Framework

### Know: 5 Key Messages (5-5-5 Rule)
| Message | ~Slides | ~Minutes |
|---------|---------|----------|
| 1. Deep Kubernetes & cloud platform expertise (10+ years) | 5 | 5 |
| 2. Proven ability to scale systems from startup to enterprise | 5 | 5 |
| 3. Red Hat senior leadership background (credibility) | 5 | 5 |
| 4. Open source contributor & thought leader | 5 | 5 |
| 5. Collaborative problem-solver who unblocks teams | 5 | 5 |
| **Total** | **~25** | **~25** |

### Feel
Build from skepticism → confidence → eagerness:
- **Skepticism phase (opening):** "Why should we hire a consultant/specialist?"
- **Confidence phase (sections 1-3):** "This person has solved problems we care about at scale"
- **Credibility phase (sections 4-5):** "They're not just smart—they're collaborative and invested in community"

### Do
Schedule a conversation with Paul about your platform challenges. Explore a potential engagement or hire.

## Audience Profile
- **Who:** Engineering leaders, CTOs, hiring managers evaluating specialized expertise
- **Technical depth:** Expert/architect level (they speak Kubernetes, cloud, distributed systems)
- **Existing knowledge:** Aware of platform challenges but want evidence of deep expertise
- **Constraints:** Skeptical of "resume inflation"; want proof of scale and pragmatism

## Talk Structure
- **Length:** 15-20 minutes (lightning + brief Q&A)
- **Flow:** Problem → Solution → Proof
  - **Problem (opening):** Platform challenges grow with scale; you need expertise
  - **Solution (sections 1-4):** Here's the expertise Paul brings
  - **Proof (section 5):** Evidence of collaboration and thought leadership

## Narrative Framework
SCR (Situation-Complication-Resolution)
- **Why SCR:** Audience is time-constrained and ready to engage if convinced. No need for SCQA.

## Pyramid Principle: Hierarchical Organization

**Apex (main idea):** Paul is a proven platform architect who can scale your infrastructure and unblock your team.

**Arguments (tier 2 — the 5 key messages):**
1. 10+ years Kubernetes/cloud expertise (deep technical credibility)
2. Scaled systems from startup phase to enterprise (pragmatic experience)
3. Red Hat principal engineer background (institutional credibility)
4. Open source contributions and thought leadership (community credibility)
5. Collaborative problem-solver with team-focused mindset (culture fit)

**Evidence (tier 3):**
- Specific projects scaled (complexity, outcome, team size)
- Open source contributions (projects, impact)
- Speaking engagements, writing, community presence
- Team feedback / testimonials

## Key Stories & Examples
- **Opening story:** A platform problem that required architecture expertise to unblock (scale, performance, ops)
- **Scaling example:** Took a system from 10 engineers to 100+ engineers; what had to change
- **OSS contribution:** Major project Paul contributed to; why it matters
- **Leadership example:** How Paul helped a team level up their platform thinking
- **Collaboration story:** Example of working cross-functionally to unblock a team

## Visual Style
- **Tone:** Professional, humble, approachable (not self-promotional)
- **Red Hat branding:** Appropriate but not overused
- **Emphasis:** Balanced (data points, stories, visuals)
- **Mood:** "Here's what I've learned; here's how I can help"

## Diagrams & Animations
- **Mermaid:** Timeline of scale growth, architecture evolution
- **Vue animations:** Optional—keep this lightweight for a lightning talk
- **Approach:** Simple, data-driven visuals (charts of growth, timeline of projects)

## Success Criteria
- Attendees remember Paul's name and at least one project he's led
- At least 3 people schedule a follow-up conversation
- Feedback emphasizes credibility and approachability

## Reference Materials
- Paul's GitHub (projects, contributions)
- Red Hat tenure and achievements
- Open source projects (Kubernetes ecosystem, infrastructure tools)
- Blog posts or conference talks
- Team testimonials
